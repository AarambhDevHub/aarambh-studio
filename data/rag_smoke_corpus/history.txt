Aarambh Studio — History Reference

The Roman Empire was founded in 27 BC by Augustus Caesar after the fall of
the Roman Republic. The western Roman Empire fell in 476 AD when Romulus
Augustulus was deposed by the Germanic king Odoacer. The eastern Roman
Empire, also known as the Byzantine Empire, continued until 1453 AD when
Constantinople fell to the Ottoman Empire.

The Industrial Revolution began in Britain in the late 18th century. It
was marked by the transition from hand production methods to machines, the
development of the steam engine, and the rise of the factory system. The
Industrial Revolution spread to continental Europe and North America in
the 19th century.

The Renaissance was a period of cultural rebirth in Europe spanning the
14th to 17th centuries. It began in Italy, particularly in Florence, and
later spread throughout Europe. The Renaissance is associated with a
revival of classical learning, art, and scientific inquiry.
