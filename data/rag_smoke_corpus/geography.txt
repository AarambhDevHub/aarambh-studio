Aarambh Studio — Geography Reference

The capital of France is Paris. Paris is located in the north-central part
of the country on the Seine river. France is a member of the European Union
and uses the euro as its currency.

The capital of Japan is Tokyo. Tokyo is located on the eastern coast of the
island of Honshu. Japan is an archipelago nation in East Asia and uses the
yen as its currency.

The capital of Brazil is Brasilia. Brasilia is located in the interior of
the country, far from the coast. Brazil is the largest country in South
America and uses the real as its currency.
